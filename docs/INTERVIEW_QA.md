# Interview Q&A

**Why deterministic matching first?** It handles obvious cases cheaply and makes common decisions explainable.

**Why integer paise?** Financial policy should not depend on floating-point rounding.

**Why can an LLM diagnose but not recover?** Diagnosis is advisory; authorization has direct economic consequences and belongs in deterministic policy code.

**What is missing?** Real source adapters, durable audit storage, authentication, production-scale reconciliation, and payment execution. None are claimed here.
